reVCS 0.55 Alpha Update 8.6 - cumulative update

- Add a redesigned native PC menu using the original VCS fonts, a latest-valid-save
  Continue option and keyboard, mouse and controller navigation.
- Add a blurred gameplay background to pause menus and improve text readability
  and spacing.
- Add quiet instrumental music to the main and pause menus, with smooth fades,
  uninterrupted tab navigation and control through the Music Volume slider.
- Add station glyphs to Audio settings and the in-car station-change display,
  including User Tracks and Radio Off, with a larger selected-station preview.
- Extend road GPS to active mission targets and the full map. Custom routes are
  pink; mission routes are gold.
- Make Direct3D 11 the primary x64 renderer, retaining Direct3D 9 as the x86
  compatibility fallback. Add adjustable PS2 VCS/Enhanced colour filters and
  optional experimental D3D11 radiosity, disabled by default.
- Add -nointro to skip the intro movie and splash while retaining the startup
  notice. Restyle the English, Portuguese and Russian notices to match the menus.
- Correct helicopter magnet/cargo constraints after Bullet physics updates and
  extend the magnet cable from approximately 4 m to 6 m.
- Improve ambient ambulance and fire-engine responses, including revival and
  continued water-cannon use after the crew leaves the vehicle.
- Correct detailed-vehicle fading, the Wintergreen driver's seating position
  and the heavy-weapon idle pose.
- Include the earlier Update 8.1-8.5 corrections and features: helicopter and
  fire-truck fixes, forklift pickups, stunt cameras, traffic lights, EU/US save
  compatibility, adaptive streaming, mip-chain support and the texture-pack view.

This full cumulative package updates installations made by the first public
installer (Alpha Update 8 / 8.0) and Updates 8.1-8.5 directly to 8.6. Intermediate
updates are not required. Original scripts, regional text, saves and settings
are preserved. Experimental rendering does not claim exact PS2 visual parity.
