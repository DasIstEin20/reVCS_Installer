reVCS 0.55 Alpha Update 9
Changes since public Update 8.11

Gameplay and vehicle behaviour
- Improved NPC driving, traffic avoidance and recovery from blocked routes.
- Improved vehicle steering, stopping behaviour and navigation, including boats.
- Restored additional original VCS vehicle behaviour and wet-surface handling.
- Corrected vehicle repair and protection cheats.
- Improved unarmed combat, attack animations, footwork and hit reactions.
- Improved weapon aiming, target selection and crosshair behaviour.

HUD, map and interface
- Reworked the HUD and added adjustable HUD scaling in reVCS Extras.
- Improved health, armour, oxygen, wanted stars and ammunition displays.
- Improved radar markers, GPS waypoints and pause-map navigation.
- Added an optional map highlight for businesses under attack.
- Restored the Domestobot camera and radar behaviour.
- Improved menu navigation, statistics, fonts and interface artwork.

Saves and controls
- Added quicksave and quickload with customisable F5/F9 shortcuts.
- Improved Continue and Load Game behaviour, including quicksave support.
- Added the option to load the last quicksave after death or arrest.
- Corrected time advancement when saving at safehouses.

Audio and localisation
- Added custom music selection for the main menu, loading screens and pause menu, including User Tracks.
- Added Persian localisation and improved text rendering.
- Added Vice City Stories trivia to loading screens.

Graphics and effects
- Improved HD texture-pack support and texture handling.
- Restored additional blood, dirt, sand and water effects.
- Improved headlights, shadows and motion blur.
- Improved startup and initial cutscene loading.

Settings and diagnostics
- Consolidated game settings and input bindings into revcs.set.
- Improved crash reporting and added compressed debug logs with system information.
- Added automatic report rotation and privacy improvements. Reports are not uploaded automatically.
- Added verification method.

Release information
- Updated Direct3D 9 and Direct3D 11 builds for x86 and x64. Direct3D 11 remains the default.
- Requires Update 8.11 and reVCS Manager 1.35.15.
- Game settings will be reset to defaults. Existing saves and progress are unaffected.
- Original game resources are not included in this update.
- Some vehicle physics, shadow and texture replacement issues remain. Experimental vehicle physics options remain opt-in.
