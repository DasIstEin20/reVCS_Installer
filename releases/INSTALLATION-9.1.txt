reVCS Update 9.1 - installation

Close the game. Use reVCS Manager 1.35.16 or newer and Check Game Updates,
or Choose local ZIP update with reVCS-Update-9.1-Full.zip.

Requires an installed 0.55 Alpha Update 9 (9.0). For a fresh conversion,
apply Update 8.11, then Update 9, then Update 9.1 through Manager.
This ZIP contains source-dependent resource deltas. Do not extract it as a
manual game overlay. It is not a complete game or fresh-install converter.

Includes x86/x64 and Direct3D 9/11; root EXEs use Direct3D 11.
Your saves, settings, selected texture packs and language are preserved.
The later REVCS INTRO video and Greetings_From farewell artwork are included
as user-approved recreated presentation replacements, together with supplied
PlayStation glyph artwork. Original game resources are patched from your
existing installation. Private commercial menu music samples are not distributed.

Validation reuses completed x64 development checks for the matching engine
code, with fresh build identity, package integrity and update-apply checks.
Full mission and aiming playthroughs remain community testing.
